Project Objective

Develop a capstone system that transforms proprietary, unseen, or dense document collections into scaffolded, gamified learning experiences. The AI grounds every generated lesson and quiz in the user's uploaded documents — not general knowledge — using RAG to prevent hallucination and ensure fidelity to source material.

Scope targets a complete end-to-end pipeline: document ingestion → curriculum decomposition → interactive slide deck generation → quiz-gated mastery progression — all within the capstone timeline.
